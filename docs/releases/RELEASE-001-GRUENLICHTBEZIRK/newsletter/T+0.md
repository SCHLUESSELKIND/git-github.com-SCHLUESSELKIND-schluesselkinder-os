## Production notes

- The five links are the message. No paragraphs.

  Bandcamp present because the SNUFFRAGGA audience overweights these.
- All URLs MUST be replaced with real ones before send. Default `<id>` placeholders
  will visibly break the message — that's the failsafe (don't auto-send).
- No tracking pixels. No UTM parameters. Privacy is brand.
- Use Listmonk's "link click" tracking AT MOST for the shop link, since that's the
  one with a measurable conversion. Streaming-service clicks: untracked.
- Send window: 09:00 CET. Late enough that the release has propagated globally,
  early enough to hit European morning commute / first-coffee.

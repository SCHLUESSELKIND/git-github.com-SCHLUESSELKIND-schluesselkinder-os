# Public Brand System

## Strategic Direction

- SCHLUESSELKINDER is not horror, not cyberpunk, not fetish decoration, and not an AI moodboard.
- The brand is a cold underground music and streetwear label system.
- Core tension: post-club melancholy, brutalist fashion culture, ritual restraint, and techno-industrial emptiness.
- Use radical reduction. Do not add visual motifs just because they are available.
- Every public page should pass this filter: would an obscure Berlin underground label actually publish this?

## Visual Decision Filter

1. Does it make the brand more iconic?
2. Does it increase tension without adding noise?
3. Does it feel expensive, cold, and controlled?
4. Does it avoid explaining too much?
5. Does it protect the chair and rune system?

## Color Tokens

- `black`: `#070605` for the site ground.
- `black-panel`: `#11100d` for concrete-like surfaces.
- `off-white`: `#f2eee6` for primary typography.
- `concrete`: `#a9a096` for secondary text.
- `deep-red`: `#8f1117` for accents, signals, and active states.
- `border`: stone-dark borders from the Tailwind stone scale.

## Typography Principles

- Use large uppercase display type as the main image system.
- Keep letter spacing at `0`; avoid decorative tracking.
- Use short bilingual fragments rather than paragraphs.
- Treat track titles and manifesto lines as artifacts.
- Avoid startup copy, slogans, jokes, and explanatory feature language.

## Spacing Principles

- Use cinematic whitespace and hard section rhythm.
- Prefer full-width editorial sections over cards.
- Keep mobile layouts stacked with enough vertical air.
- Use borders as structural cuts, not decoration.
- Avoid dense grids unless the content is archival.

## Component Tone

- Components are local to `apps/web` during Sprint 2 and Sprint 2.5.
- Components should be composable and blunt: header, campaign image, symbol stamp, section frame, manifest line, track list, shop preview.
- No rounded cards, soft shadows, pastel surfaces, or dashboard panels.
- Navigation should feel like a label index, not a product app.

## Symbol Rules

- The rune/key mark is a system mark, not a decorative icon.
- The rune/key symbol is the systemic identity language.
- The horn mark belongs to SHIBARI KAWAII and release artifacts.
- Use symbols as stamps, rails, edge marks, and section cuts.
- Do not repeat the same symbol more than once in a single visual field unless it is part of a deliberate rail.
- Symbols should be off-white, stone-muted, or black-on-image. Avoid bright red symbols except for rare campaign moments.

## Image Rules

- Use images as editorial evidence, not collage material.
- The dungeon/chair image is the primary recurring campaign environment.
- The fabric figure is deprecated for the primary public experience. Do not use it on public landing, artist, or shop surfaces.
- The eye/key image is a high-intensity symbol image and should be used sparingly.
- The EIN image is supporting poster texture, never the whole identity.
- The provided SHIBARI KAWAII ropeface mark (`shibari-kawaii-ropeface.png`) and chair environment are artist identity anchors.
- The provided rune/key mark (`rune-key-mark.png`) is the systemic identity mark.
- Image treatments should use hard crops, black frames, restrained overlays, and no rounded corners.
- The chair environment is the only recurring campaign image world until stronger fashion campaign assets exist.
- All other visuals are references only unless explicitly approved.

## Texture Rules

- Use CSS texture for concrete grain, line grids, hard red traces, and atmospheric restraint.
- Image assets may be used in Sprint 2.5, but only as controlled editorial anchors.
- Avoid gradient-heavy cyberpunk effects.
- No decorative orbs, glow blobs, or generic neon backgrounds.

## What To Avoid

- Corporate language.
- Startup SaaS aesthetics.
- Portfolio-page composition.
- Generic SaaS, portfolio, Shopify, or merch-shop language.
- Fake commerce states.
- Checkout, stock, cart, prices, Stripe, or Printful code.
- Meme irony or overly clever microcopy.
- Poster collage.
- Moodboard collage energy.
- Product-card language.
- Repeating all assets on every page.
- Horror props, creepy doll energy, and internet occult aesthetics.
- Overdesigned cyberpunk.

## Shop Rules

- The shop must feel like a future object archive, not a store.
- Use cold, industrial, minimal, post-club, brutal-luxury language.
- Do not imply active commerce.
- Do not show checkout, cart, prices, stock, or product availability.

## Copy Rules

- Copy must stay sparse, cold, bilingual, and editorial.
- Use short fragments, metadata, and manifest lines.
- Avoid explaining the system to the user.
- Avoid jokes, memes, feature language, and conversion copy.
